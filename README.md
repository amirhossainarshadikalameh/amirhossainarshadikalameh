# 💫 About Me:
I am an undergraduate student with a strong interest in understanding Network Dynamics and Hypergraphs and how they apply to jet tagging. I enjoy exploring how simulations and artificial intelligence can work together to solve challenging problems. My goal is to develop practical tools and methods that use AI to study and model complex physical systems. I am also interested in studying high-energy particles and have been focusing on researching jet separation with the help of AI methods like ParticleNet and ParticleTransform.<br>My primary interest lies in the exploration of high-order interaction models, particularly in the study of hypergraphs and their applications within complex physics and double-b jet tagging. I am equally fascinated by Neuroscience, with a focus on modeling neural networks using advanced approaches such as spiking neural networks (SNNs), and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and attention-based architectures like transformers, to understand complex behaviors in neural processing, learning dynamics, and memory formation within the brain.
