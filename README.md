# Hi, I'm Amirhossain Arshadi

**Physics · Complex Systems · Network Science · Quantitative Biology**

B.Sc. in Physics from Isfahan University of Technology, ranked first in my department for four years. My thesis, *Structural Complexity of Real and Model Networks*, was supervised by Prof. Farhad Shahbazi. I work where network science, statistical physics and quantitative biology meet: I test when network-complexity measures carry information beyond null models, build machine-learning surrogates for spectral properties of graphs, and model metabolic regulation in living systems.

🔭 Looking for **fully funded master's and PhD positions** in complex systems, network science and statistical physics — with applications to biological, financial and other real-world systems.

## 🔬 What I'm working on

| Project | Area | Result so far |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural complexity of real and model networks** — B.Sc. thesis, Nov 2024 – present | Complex networks | Across **20 centrality measures** and five real networks tested against null ensembles, centrality entropy tracks **degree heterogeneity** rather than higher-order structure: ER gave the highest entropy for all five, and the degree-heterogeneous ensembles came closer in four of the five |
| **Centrality correlations** — current phase of the thesis | Complex networks | Building a measure from the mean, variance and entropy of pairwise centrality correlations — structure the degree sequence alone cannot explain. Poster at the ICTP Spring College |
| **Fast spectral-gap prediction with GNNs** | Graph machine learning | A GCN on a *k*-NN "graph of graphs" predicts algebraic connectivity λ₂ from 11 cheap features — **≈ 95%** accuracy over **6,000** graphs from 3 random-graph models |
| **Metabolic feedback in *C. elegans*** | Quantitative biology | Reversible glycolysis–gluconeogenesis model linking FBP, ATP/ADP and NADH/NAD⁺ dynamics to energy balance across sleep–wake states |
| **[Time series as networks](https://github.com/amirhossainarshadikalameh/visibility-network-on-financial-market-data)** | Complex networks | Five mappings — natural and horizontal visibility, ordinal partition, quantile and proximity graphs — applied to daily stock prices |
